Capstone and showcase 35% The working controller, its benchmark and stress test, and a live demo.

AI-use policy

AI is required in this course, not just allowed. You will do nearly all of your coding by talking to an assistant.

  • Log your prompts. The prompt log is graded evidence of your process.
  • Verify everything. Output counts only after it passes a test you can explain.
  • You own the result. If the controller fails, the accountability is yours, as it would be on a plant.
  • Name your tools. State which assistant you used and where it helped or misled you.

Weekly rhythm

Session 1 is a Concept hour: the idea in plain words. Session 2 is a Studio hour: design the week’s pieces together. Session 3 is a Lab hour: build and test in the browser. Each week ends with one deliverable that feeds the next.
