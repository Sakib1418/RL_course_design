AI-use policy

AI is required in this course, not just allowed. You will write almost every line of code by describing what you want to an assistant.

  • Log your prompts. The log is graded and is how others learn from your process.
  • Verify everything against a balance, a limiting case or the data. Plausible output is not correct output.
  • You own the result. If the twin gives a wrong answer, the responsibility is yours, not the assistant’s.

Weekly rhythm

Session 1 is a Concept hour on the idea. Session 2 is a Studio hour of guided vibe coding. Session 3 is a Lab hour where you apply the week to a bioreactor or CSTR dataset. Each week ends with one deliverable that feeds the capstone.
