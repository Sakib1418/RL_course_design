## Assessment

| Component | Weight | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly labs | 35% | Seven working pieces of the agent system, each checked against a short rubric. |
| Paper discussion and build journal | 15% | Short written responses to readings, plus a running log of design decisions and dead ends. |
| Capstone proposal | 10% | A one-page plan naming your process problem, agents, tools and twin checks. |
| Capstone, showcase and technical memo | 40% | The working multi-agent assistant, a live demonstration, and a memo with evaluation and audit trail. |

## AI-use policy

AI is required in this course, not just allowed. You will build agents with an AI coding assistant, and you will learn to direct it well.

- **Log your work.** Keep your prompts and agent traces. They are part of the build journal.
- **Verify everything.** A fluent answer is not a correct one. Check claims against sources, balances and the twin.
- **You own the result.** What you submit is yours, including every error the assistant introduced and you did not catch.
- **Disclose limits.** State what your system cannot do and where it failed during evaluation.

## Readings

Readings are short and chosen for discussion, not coverage. Specific selections are provided at the start of the course.

- A seminal paper on retrieval-augmented generation
- A paper on language-model agents that reason and act with tools
- A self-driving laboratory perspective from chemistry or bioprocessing
- A regulatory or standards document on data integrity and computerized systems in GMP settings
